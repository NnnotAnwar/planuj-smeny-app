## Flagship feature: **Announcements** 📣

Managers post a short notice to the whole organization or to one location; every member of the org reads it. Fills a real gap — today there's no way for a manager to say "we're closed Monday" or "new uniforms arrived" to the team inside the app.

### Why this feature
- **Self-contained** — its own table, service, hook; does not touch timesheets / overview / scheduling internals, so it won't collide with parallel work.
- **A complete vertical slice** — DB → RLS → service → hook → UI. Perfect to grow from "render a list" into owning a whole feature end-to-end.
- Natural follow-ups already in the backlog: *broadcast + acknowledge*, *email digests* (ROADMAP.md §F).

### Data model (quick reference)
`announcements`: `id`, `organization_id`, `location_id` (null = whole org), `author_id` (SET NULL if author leaves), `title`, `body`, `pinned`, `created_at`, `updated_at`.
RLS: **read** = anyone in the org; **create/edit/delete** = managers (rank ≥ 20) in that org (+ superadmin). Same shape as `time_off`.
